# CEOCOR — Editorial humano

User delegated design and execution with “hazlo”. Working direction: an editorial portrait experience, clean warm paper, a deep green identity related to the incumbent teal, generous asymmetric split composition, Manrope lettering with a contrasting Playfair italic voice. A human portrait anchors the first viewport. Warm sunlight makes the subject tangible. Opening animation unveils the portrait; reduced motion skips it.

Visitor path: understand the clinic → explore four treatments through native disclosures → understand the approach → find location → resolve questions → prepare a WhatsApp consultation.

Signature: the smiling seal, softened portrait corner and discreet overlapping clinical/human caption. Full page alternates light, sage and deep green without repeated card grids.

Risk: campaign portrait and clinic interior are generated illustrative assets, labeled in their captions. No fabricated staff, patient results, testimonials, qualifications or performance statistics.

Quality requirements: high quality composition at 1440 and 390 px, actual user viewport, no horizontal overflow, accessible disclosures, contact action opens a prepared message without silently transmitting personal information. Full keyboard navigation, reduced motion, local font assets.
